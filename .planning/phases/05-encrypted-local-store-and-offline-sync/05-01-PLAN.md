<objective>
Add the agent's encrypted SQLite store (outbox, replay cache, persisted trust sequence floors, trust and policy cache) with a separate DPAPI/0600-protected store key, implement rmm-trust's SeqStore and ReplayCache on it, and use it for enrollment trust verification.

Purpose: Everything the agent must remember across restarts and outages lives here, encrypted at rest.

- Duplicate msg_id push is a no-op returning true
- Reopening the store after a crash keeps every row whose insert committed
